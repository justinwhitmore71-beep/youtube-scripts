## Description
Anna makes a higher share of close shots AND long shots than Ben, but Ben has the higher overall percentage (82% vs 39%). The reason is the mix: Anna mostly takes hard long shots and Ben mostly takes easy close ones. This reversal is called Simpson's paradox. The numbers are an invented example.

## Pinned comment (suggested)
Nobody's maths is wrong here: both totals are correct. They just answer different questions. Comparing like with like (close vs close, long vs long), Anna is the better shooter; the overall averages mostly tell you who took the easier shots. (The numbers are made up to keep the arithmetic simple.)
