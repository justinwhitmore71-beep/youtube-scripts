[HOOK] A steel bolt sinks, but a huge ship of the same steel floats. {c2}
[MASCOT: Steel should sink!] Shouldn't steel sink straight to the bottom?
Water pushes up with a force equal to the weight of water pushed aside. {c1}
[EQUATION: F_buoyant = weight of water pushed aside] A steel lump pushes aside a little water, so it sinks. {c4}
Shape it into a hollow hull, and it pushes aside far more water at the same weight. {c2}
When the pushed-aside water matches the ship's weight, it floats. {c1} {c3}
[TAKEAWAY] Ships float by shape: steel plus air averages out lighter than water. {c3}
