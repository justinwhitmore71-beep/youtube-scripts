[HOOK] Squash foil into a tight ball and it sinks; fold it into a tray and it floats. {c2}
[MASCOT: Same foil, how?] Same foil, same weight: how?
[EQUATION: F_buoyant = weight of water pushed aside] Water pushes up with a force equal to the weight of the water you push aside. {c1}
The ball pushes aside only its own small volume. {c2}
The tray pushes aside a big hollow volume, so the push up grows. {c2}
It settles once it has pushed aside its own weight of water. {c3}
[TAKEAWAY] That's how steel ships float: the shape, not the metal, decides. {c2} {c3}
