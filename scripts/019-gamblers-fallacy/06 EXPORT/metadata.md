## Description
After five heads in a row, a fair coin's next flip is still 50-50, because each flip is independent of the ones before. Six heads in a row and five heads followed by a tail are equally likely, 1 in 64 each: they share the first five flips and differ only in the last one. Expecting tails to be 'due' is the gambler's fallacy.
