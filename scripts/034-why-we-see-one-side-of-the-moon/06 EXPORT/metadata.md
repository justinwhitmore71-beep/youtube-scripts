## Description
The Moon spins on its axis exactly once for every trip around Earth, about 27.3 days each, so the same face always points at us. If it didn't spin at all, its far side would swing into view halfway round. The far side is hidden by the spin, not by a lack of it.

## Pinned comment (suggested)
The far side isn't permanently dark: at new moon, when we can't see the Moon at all, the side facing away from us is the one in sunlight. 'Far side' just means the side we never see.
