[HOOK] A bet that pays four to one on a die roll still loses you money. {c3}
[MASCOT: Four to one sounds great!] Four dollars for one sounds great, right?
Roll a six and win four dollars; roll anything else and lose one. {c3}
[EQUATION: EV = 4 × 1/6 − 1 × 5/6 = −1/6] Expected value weighs each result by its chance: four sixths minus five sixths. {c1} {c3}
That's minus one sixth: about seventeen cents lost every roll, on average. {c2} {c3}
You win big but rarely, and lose small five times as often. {c3}
[TAKEAWAY] Judge a bet by expected value, not payout: the long run always collects. {c1} {c2}
