# Script B: The fair price is five

Angle: find the payout that breaks even

[HOOK] On a die roll, four-to-one on a six loses money; five-to-one would be fair. {c3} {c4}
[MASCOT: Why five?] Why exactly five?
[EQUATION: EV = 5 × 1/6 − 1 × 5/6 = 0] Expected value adds each result times its chance. {c1}
A six comes once in six rolls; the other five rolls each cost you a dollar. {c3}
To break even, that one win must pay back all five losses: five dollars. {c3} {c4}
Pay only four, and you lose a dollar every six rolls on average. {c2} {c3}
[TAKEAWAY] Fair odds match the payout to the chances; anything less quietly drains you. {c1} {c2}
