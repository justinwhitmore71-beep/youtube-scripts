[HOOK] Multiply any point by i, and it swings a quarter turn around zero. {c3}
[MASCOT: Multiplying makes things spin?] How can multiplying make something spin?
Ordinary numbers run left to right; imaginary numbers, multiples of i, run up. {c2}
[EQUATION: i(2 + i) = 2i + i² = −1 + 2i] Take two plus i, and multiply by i: two i plus i squared. {c4}
i squared is minus one, so that's minus one plus two i. {c1} {c4}
The parts swapped places and one flipped sign, exactly what a quarter turn does. {c4}
[TAKEAWAY] i isn't a fake number; it's a turn you can multiply by. {c3}
