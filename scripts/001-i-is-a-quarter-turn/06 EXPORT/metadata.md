5. Multiply by i, watch the point spin

## Description
Multiplying by i turns a point on the complex plane a quarter turn around zero. Multiplying by minus one flips a point to the other side of zero, a half turn, so two quarter turns give minus one: that's why i × i = −1. Here ordinary numbers run left to right and imaginary numbers run up and down.

## Pinned comment (suggested)
"Imaginary" is just a name: on the number plane, i is simply the point one step straight up from zero. Multiplying by i turns every point a quarter turn counterclockwise around zero, not only the ones in the video.
