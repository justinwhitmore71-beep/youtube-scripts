## Description
At the space station's height, about 400 km up, gravity is still about 8.7 m/s², almost 90% as strong as on the ground, because that height barely changes the distance from Earth's centre. Astronauts float because they and the station are falling together. The station moves sideways at nearly 8 km/s, so Earth curves away beneath it as fast as it falls.
