## Description
Light is fast, about 300,000 km every second, but not instant, and the Sun is about 150 million km away. Distance over speed gives about 500 seconds, a bit over 8 minutes, so the Sun you see is always the Sun of 8 minutes ago. The farther you look, the older the light: from distant galaxies, it set off millions of years ago.

## Pinned comment (suggested)
The 8 minutes is for light: it's how long sunlight already on its way would keep arriving. And 150 million km is the AVERAGE Earth-Sun distance, so the exact delay is a little different at different times of year.
