[HOOK] Give me any list of primes, and I can always find one you missed. {c3}
[MASCOT: Even a huge list?] Even if the list is enormous?
[EQUATION: 2 × 3 × 5 × 7 + 1 = 211] Take two, three, five and seven: multiply and add one to get two hundred eleven. {c4}
Two hundred eleven leaves remainder one when divided by any of them. {c4}
So its prime factors, and it must have some, are all new. {c2} {c3}
Here it's a new prime itself; other times it splits into new primes. {c4}
[TAKEAWAY] However long your list, a prime hides beyond it, so primes never end. {c3}
