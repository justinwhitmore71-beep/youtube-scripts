What times zero makes ten?

the wrong guess is 'infinity'

1[HOOK] Ten divided by zero isn't infinity; it has no answer at all.

2[MASCOT: Isn't it infinity?] Wait, isn't it just infinity?

3 Division undoes multiplication: ten divided by two is five, because five times two is ten.

4[EQUATION: 10 ÷ 0 = ? → ? × 0 = 10] So ten divided by zero asks: what times zero makes ten?

5 But anything times zero is zero, so no number works.

6 Tiny positive divisors shoot up, tiny negative ones shoot down: no single answer.

7[TAKEAWAY] Division by zero is undefined, because it asks a question no number can answer.

Script B

How many zeros fit in twelve?

division as 'how many fit'
82 words, about 36 s

1[HOOK] Dividing by zero isn't forbidden by a rule; it's a question with no answer.

2[MASCOT: Why not just call it infinity?] Can't we just say it's infinity?

3 Twelve divided by three asks how many threes fit in twelve: four.

4[EQUATION: 12 − 0 − 0 − 0 … = 12] Twelve divided by zero asks how many zeros fit in twelve.

5 Take away zero, again and again, and you never get any closer to empty.

6 Infinity doesn't help either: from the negative side the answers plunge the other way.

7[TAKEAWAY] There's no number that fits, so division by zero is undefined.
