[HOOK] Dividing by zero isn't forbidden by a rule; it's a question with no answer. {c1}
[MASCOT: Why not just call it infinity?] Can't we just say it's infinity?
Twelve divided by three asks how many threes fit in twelve: four. {c5}
[EQUATION: 12 − 0 − 0 − 0 … = 12] Twelve divided by zero asks how many zeros fit in twelve. {c5}
Take away zero, again and again, and you never get any closer to empty. {c5}
Infinity doesn't help either: from the negative side the answers plunge the other way. {c4}
[TAKEAWAY] There's no number that fits, so division by zero is undefined. {c1}
