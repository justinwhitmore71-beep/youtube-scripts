# Script A: What times zero makes ten?
Series: Simple Proofs
Angle: the wrong guess is 'infinity'

[HOOK] Ten divided by zero isn't infinity; it has no answer at all. {c1}
[MASCOT: Isn't it infinity?] Wait, isn't it just infinity?
Division undoes multiplication: ten divided by two is five, because five times two is ten. {c2}
[EQUATION: 10 ÷ 0 = ?  →  ? × 0 = 10] So ten divided by zero asks: what times zero makes ten? {c2}
But anything times zero is zero, so no number works. {c3}
Tiny positive divisors shoot up, tiny negative ones shoot down: no single answer. {c4}
[TAKEAWAY] Division by zero is undefined, because it asks a question no number can answer. {c1}
