## Description
A logarithm is an exponent: log of 1000 is 3 because 10³ = 1000. Multiplying powers of ten adds their exponents, 10² × 10³ = 10⁵, so the log of a product is the sum of the logs: log(100 × 1000) = 2 + 3. The same rule, log(MN) = log M + log N, holds for any positive numbers.

## Pinned comment (suggested)
This works for numbers that aren't powers of ten too: log 2 + log 3 = log 6, because 2 and 3 are also powers of ten, just with fractional exponents (about 10^0.301 and 10^0.477).
