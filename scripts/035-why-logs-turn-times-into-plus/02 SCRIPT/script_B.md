[HOOK] The log of a product is the sum of the logs, not their product. {c2}
[MASCOT: That breaks the pattern!] Why plus, when the numbers are multiplied?
For powers of ten, the log is just the number of zeros: a thousand has three. {c1}
[EQUATION: log(M × N) = log M + log N] A thousand times a hundred is a hundred thousand: five zeros. {c3}
Three zeros plus two zeros makes five, because multiplying stacks the zeros together. {c3}
That rule holds for all positive numbers, not just powers of ten. {c2}
[TAKEAWAY] Logs count tens multiplied together, so multiplying adds logs. {c1} {c2}
