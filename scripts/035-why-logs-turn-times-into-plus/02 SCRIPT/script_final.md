[HOOK] Logarithms turn every multiplication into an addition. {c2}
[MASCOT: Isn't it log a times log b?] Shouldn't the log of a product be a product of logs?
A log answers one question: what power of ten gives this number? {c1}
So log of a hundred is two, and log of a thousand is three. {c1}
[EQUATION: 10² × 10³ = 10⁵  so  log(100 × 1000) = 2 + 3] A hundred times a thousand is ten squared times ten cubed: ten to the five. {c3}
Multiplying powers adds the exponents, so the logs add: two plus three is five. {c2} {c3}
[TAKEAWAY] Logs are exponents, so they turn times into plus. {c1} {c2}
