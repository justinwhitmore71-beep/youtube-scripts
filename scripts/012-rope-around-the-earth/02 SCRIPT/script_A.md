[HOOK] Wrap a rope around Earth, add one meter, and it rises sixteen centimeters all the way round. {c3}
[MASCOT: One meter? Over the whole planet?] Wait, one meter spread over the whole planet?
A circle's length is two pi times its radius. {c1}
[EQUATION: Δr = ΔC / 2π = 1 m / 2π ≈ 16 cm] So each extra meter of rope adds one over two pi meters of radius. {c1} {c3}
That's about sixteen centimeters, and Earth's size never appears. {c3}
Do the same around a basketball, and you get the same sixteen-centimeter gap. {c3}
[TAKEAWAY] The gap depends on the extra rope, not the size of the circle. {c3}
