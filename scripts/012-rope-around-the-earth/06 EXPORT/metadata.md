## Description
Wrap a rope around Earth's equator, then add just one meter. Held evenly, it rises about 16 cm off the ground all the way round. A circle's radius is its length divided by 2π, so one extra meter always adds 1 ÷ 2π ≈ 0.16 m of radius, and Earth's size cancels out. A basketball gets the same 16 cm gap.

## Pinned comment (suggested)
This assumes the longer rope is held in a perfect circle, the same height off the ground all the way round, and treats Earth as a perfect sphere. The 16 cm comes from 1 m ÷ 2π, and Earth's size cancels out of the sum.
