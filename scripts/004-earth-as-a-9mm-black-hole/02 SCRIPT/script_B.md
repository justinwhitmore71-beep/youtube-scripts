[HOOK] Earth would become a black hole if you squeezed it to just nine millimeters in radius. {c2}
[MASCOT: Only nine millimeters?] Why such a tiny number?
A black hole is mass packed inside a radius where not even light gets out. {c1}
[EQUATION: R = 2GM / c²] That radius is two, times gravity's constant, times the mass, over light speed squared. {c1}
Light speed squared is huge, so the radius comes out small. {c3}
Even the Sun would only need to shrink to about three kilometers. {c4}
[TAKEAWAY] Any mass can make a black hole; what matters is the squeeze. {c1}
