[MASCOT: Aren't black holes huge?] Wait, don't black holes have to be enormous?
A black hole, where not even light escapes, just needs mass squeezed inside one radius. {c1}
[EQUATION: R = 2GM / c²] That radius is two, times gravity's constant, times the mass, over light speed squared. {c1}
Light speed squared is so huge that the radius comes out tiny. {c3}

[TAKEAWAY] Black holes aren't about being big; they're about being squeezed. {c1}
