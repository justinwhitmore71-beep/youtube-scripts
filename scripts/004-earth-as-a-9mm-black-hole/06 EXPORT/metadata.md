## Description
A black hole's size depends only on its mass: R = 2GM/c². The speed of light squared is a huge number on the bottom, so the radius is tiny: about 9 mm for Earth's mass and about 3 km for the Sun's. This is a thought experiment; nothing known could actually crush Earth that far.
