# Script A: Sample size, not country size

[HOOK] Ask a thousand random people, and you can gauge a whole country within about three percent. {c1} {c2}
[MASCOT: One thousand out of millions?] Surely a big country needs a bigger sample?
A random sample's error depends on how many you ask, not how many exist. {c1}
[EQUATION: margin ≈ 2 × 0.5 / √n] The typical error shrinks with the square root of the sample size. {c3}
With a thousand people, that's about three points either way. {c2}
That holds for a small town or a country of millions. {c1}
[TAKEAWAY] The size of the sample sets the error, not the size of the country. {c1}
