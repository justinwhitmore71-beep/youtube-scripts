Sample size, not country size

the wrong guess is 'bigger country, bigger poll'
85 words, about 38 s

1[HOOK] Ask a thousand random people, and you can gauge a whole country within about three percent.

2[MASCOT: One thousand out of millions?] Surely a big country needs a bigger sample?

3 A random sample's error depends on how many you ask, not how many exist.

4[EQUATION: margin ≈ 2 × 0.5 / √n] The typical error shrinks with the square root of the sample size.

5 With a thousand people, that's about three points either way.

7[TAKEAWAY] The size of the sample sets the error, not the size of the country.
