## Description
Ask 1,000 people chosen at random and your estimate of a percentage is usually within about 3 points of the truth, whether the population is a city or a country of 100 million. A random sample's error depends on how many you ask (it shrinks with the square root of the sample size), not on how many people exist.

## Pinned comment (suggested)
Two big conditions: the 1,000 must be RANDOM (a huge biased sample doesn't help), and ±3 points is how far off it usually is, about 95% of the time, not a guarantee. Real polls also have to deal with people who don't answer.
