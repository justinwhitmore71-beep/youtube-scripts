# Script A: Squared means four times

[HOOK] Drive twice as fast, and braking takes four times the distance. {c4}
[MASCOT: Shouldn't it be twice?] Twice the speed, twice the distance, surely?
[EQUATION: KE = ½ m v²] A moving car's energy is half its mass times its speed squared. {c1}
Squared means double the speed gives four times the energy. {c1} {c4}
The same brakes on the same road remove that energy at a steady rate per meter. {c2}
So four times the energy takes four times the meters to stop. {c2} {c4}
[TAKEAWAY] Stopping distance grows with speed squared, so a little extra speed costs a lot. {c4}
