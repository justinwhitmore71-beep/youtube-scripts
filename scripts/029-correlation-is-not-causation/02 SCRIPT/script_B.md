[HOOK] Two numbers can rise and fall in step without either one causing the other. {c1}
[MASCOT: Then what's linking them?] Then what's linking them?
Say umbrella sales and traffic jams both jump on the same days. {c3}
[EQUATION: rain → umbrellas,  rain → traffic jams] Rain drives both, so they rise together even though neither causes the other. {c2}
Statisticians call that hidden driver a confounding variable. {c2}
To test a real cause, you assign it at random, which spreads the hidden drivers evenly across both groups. {c4}
[TAKEAWAY] Moving together is a clue, not proof of cause. {c1}
