A clue, not proof

3 Say umbrella sales and traffic jams both jump on the same days.

4[EQUATION: rain → umbrellas, rain → traffic jams] Rain drives both, so they rise together even though neither causes the other.

5 Statisticians call that hidden driver a confounding variable.

6 To test a real cause, you assign it at random, which spreads the hidden drivers evenly across both groups.
