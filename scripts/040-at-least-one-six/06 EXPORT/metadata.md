## Description
Roll a die four times: the chance of at least one six is only about 52%, not 4 × 1/6 = 2/3. Adding chances counts rolls with two sixes twice. Instead, find the chance of no six at all, (5/6)⁴ ≈ 48%, and subtract from 1. Even six rolls leave about a one-in-three chance of never seeing a six.

## Pinned comment (suggested)
The adding method fails fast: with seven rolls it would give 7/6, more than 100%, which can't be a probability. The 'chance of none' method never breaks: 1 − (5/6)ⁿ creeps toward 100% but never reaches it.
