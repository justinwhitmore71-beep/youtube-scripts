## Description
Put $1 at 100% interest for a year and add the interest n times: you get (1 + 1/n)^n dollars. Yearly gives $2, monthly about $2.61, daily about $2.71, and even every second stays under $2.72. Each extra split earns interest on interest, but the slices get thinner, so the total levels off at e ≈ 2.718.

## Pinned comment (suggested)
This is 100% interest for one year, split into n equal slices; real banks pay far less, and with rate r the ceiling is e^r instead of e. More compounding always helps a little, it just never helps without limit.
