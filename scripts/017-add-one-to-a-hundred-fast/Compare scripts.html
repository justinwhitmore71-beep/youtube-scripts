Script A

Pair the ends

the pairs never change
83 words, about 37 s

1[HOOK] Add every number from one to a hundred, and the answer takes five seconds.

2[MASCOT: That's a hundred additions!] Isn't that a hundred separate additions?

3 Pair the ends: one plus a hundred makes a hundred and one.

4 Two plus ninety-nine: a hundred and one again, and so on.

5[EQUATION: 50 × 101 = 5050] Each step in adds one and removes one, so every pair stays a hundred and one.

6 Fifty pairs of a hundred and one is five thousand fifty.

7[TAKEAWAY] Pair the opposite ends, and a long sum turns into one multiplication.

1[HOOK] One to a hundred adds to five thousand fifty, and one picture shows why.

2[MASCOT: Did someone add them all?] Did someone really add them one by one?

3 Write the numbers forward, then backward underneath.

4[EQUATION: 1 + 2 + ... + n = n(n + 1)/2] Each column, one over a hundred, two over ninety-nine, adds to a hundred and one.

5 A hundred columns of a hundred and one is ten thousand one hundred.

6 That's the sum written twice, so halve it: five thousand fifty.

7[TAKEAWAY] Any evenly spaced run sums to how many, times first plus last, over two.
