Divide it by itself

the two ways of dividing must agree
83 words, about 37 s

1[HOOK] Any number to the power zero is one, and dividing proves it.

2[MASCOT: Shouldn't it be zero?] Shouldn't zero copies make zero?

3 A power counts copies: five cubed is five times five times five.

4 Divide five cubed by five cubed, and every copy cancels, leaving one.

5[EQUATION: 5³ / 5³ = 5³⁻³ = 5⁰] But dividing powers subtracts the counts: three minus three is zero.

6 The same division gives one and five to the zero, so they must be equal, for any number except zero.

7[TAKEAWAY] Power zero means every copy cancelled, and what's left is one.

Script B

Walk down the powers

each step down divides by the base
83 words, about 37 s

1[HOOK] Two to the power zero is one, not zero, and you can watch it happen.

2[MASCOT: Zero twos make zero!] Wait, zero twos multiplied should give zero, surely?

3 Two cubed is two times two times two: eight.

4 Drop the power by one and you divide by two: four, then two.

5[EQUATION: 2³ = 8, 2² = 4, 2¹ = 2, 2⁰ = 1] One more step down: two divided by two is one.

6 So two to the zero is one, and the same works for any number except zero.

7[TAKEAWAY] Power zero isn't nothing; it's what's left after dividing away every copy.
