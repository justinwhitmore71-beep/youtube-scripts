# Script A: Divide it by itself
Series: Simple Proofs
Angle: the two ways of dividing must agree
Suggested [MINE] line: line 4 (rewrite it in your own words, or keep it, and put [MINE] at its start; keep its beat words so the animation still lands)

[HOOK] Any number to the power zero is one, and dividing proves it. {c2}
[MASCOT: Shouldn't it be zero?] Shouldn't zero copies make zero?
A power counts copies: five cubed is five times five times five. {c1}
Divide five cubed by five cubed, and every copy cancels, leaving one. {c3}
[EQUATION: 5³ / 5³ = 5³⁻³ = 5⁰] But dividing powers subtracts the counts: three minus three is zero. {c3}
The same division gives one and five to the zero, so they must be equal, for any number except zero. {c3} {c2}
[TAKEAWAY] Power zero means every copy cancelled, and what's left is one. {c2}
