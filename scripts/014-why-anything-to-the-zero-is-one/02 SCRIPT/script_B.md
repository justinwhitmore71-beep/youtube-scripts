[HOOK] Two to the power zero is one, not zero, and you can watch it happen. {c2}
[MASCOT: Zero twos make zero!] Wait, zero twos multiplied should give zero, surely?
Two cubed is two times two times two: eight. {c1}
Drop the power by one and you divide by two: four, then two. {c4}
[EQUATION: 2³ = 8, 2² = 4, 2¹ = 2, 2⁰ = 1] One more step down: two divided by two is one. {c4}
So two to the zero is one, and the same works for any number except zero. {c2} {c4}
[TAKEAWAY] Power zero isn't nothing; it's what's left after dividing away every copy. {c2}
