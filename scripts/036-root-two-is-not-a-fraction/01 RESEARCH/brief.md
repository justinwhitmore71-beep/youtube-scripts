# Brief: why no fraction equals the square root of 2

Series: Simple Proofs
Idea: If √2 = a/b in lowest terms, then a² = 2b², so a is even; writing a = 2k gives b² = 2k², so b is even too, contradicting 'lowest terms'. So no fraction equals √2: it is irrational.
Misconception: With big enough whole numbers, some fraction must hit √2 exactly; it's just a long decimal.
Hook angle: No fraction, however huge its numbers, equals the square root of two.
Narrowed to: √2 only, proof by contradiction (A) or by counting factors of 2 (B). No other roots, no history.
Key condition to say aloud: Fractions of whole numbers (integers).
