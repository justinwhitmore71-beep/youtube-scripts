# Script B: The twos never balance
Series: Simple Proofs
Angle: count the factors of 2 in a square
Suggested [MINE] line: line 4 (rewrite it in your own words, or keep it, and put [MINE] at its start; keep its beat words so the animation still lands)

[HOOK] You'll never find a whole-number square that's exactly twice another one. {c5}
[MASCOT: Never? There are infinitely many!] Not even among infinitely many squares?
Break any number into prime factors, and squaring doubles every count. {c6}
So a perfect square always has an even number of twos inside it. {c5}
[EQUATION: a² = 2b²] But two times a square has an odd number of twos, so a squared can't equal two b squared. {c5}
That's exactly why no fraction a over b squares to two. {c3}
[TAKEAWAY] The square root of two is irrational, because the twos can never balance. {c1} {c3}
