Script A

Both even: contradiction

the classic proof in four moves
83 words, about 37 s

1[HOOK] No fraction, however huge its numbers, equals the square root of two.

2[MASCOT: Big enough numbers must work!] Surely some giant fraction works?

3 Suppose a over b works, in lowest terms.

4[EQUATION: a² = 2b²] Then a squared equals two b squared, so a squared is even.

5 Odd numbers have odd squares, so a must be even: two k.

6 Then b squared is two k squared, so b is even too.

7 Both even means it wasn't in lowest terms, so no fraction works.

8[TAKEAWAY] Root two is irrational: its decimal never ends or repeats.

Script B

The twos never balance

count the factors of 2 in a square
85 words, about 38 s

1[HOOK] You'll never find a whole-number square that's exactly twice another one.

2[MASCOT: Never? There are infinitely many!] Not even among infinitely many squares?

3 Break any number into prime factors, and squaring doubles every count.

4 So a perfect square always has an even number of twos inside it.

5[EQUATION: a² = 2b²] But two times a square has an odd number of twos, so a squared can't equal two b squared.

6 That's exactly why no fraction a over b squares to two.

7[TAKEAWAY] The square root of two is irrational, because the twos can never balance.
