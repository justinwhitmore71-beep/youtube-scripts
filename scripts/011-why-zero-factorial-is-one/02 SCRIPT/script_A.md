# Script A: Walk down the staircase

[HOOK] Zero factorial is one, not zero, and a staircase proves it. {c2}
[MASCOT: Shouldn't that be zero?] Wait, shouldn't nothing multiplied be zero?
Four factorial means four times three times two times one: twenty-four. {c1}
[EQUATION: n! = n × (n − 1)!] Each factorial is its number times the factorial below it. {c3}
So stepping down means dividing: twenty-four over four is six, over three is two, over two is one. {c3} {c4}
One more step: one divided by one leaves zero factorial equal to one. {c4}
[TAKEAWAY] Zero factorial is one because it's the only value that keeps the staircase going. {c2} {c4}
