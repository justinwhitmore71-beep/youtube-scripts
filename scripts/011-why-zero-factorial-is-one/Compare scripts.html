Walk down the staircase

divide your way down to 0!

1[HOOK] Zero factorial is one, not zero, and a staircase proves it.

2[MASCOT: Shouldn't that be zero?] Wait, shouldn't nothing multiplied be zero?

3 Four factorial means four times three times two times one: twenty-four.

4[EQUATION: n! = n × (n − 1)!] Each factorial is its number times the factorial below it.

5 So stepping down means dividing: twenty-four over four is six, over three is two, over two is one.

6 One more step: one divided by one leaves zero factorial equal to one.

7[TAKEAWAY] Zero factorial is one because it's the only value that keeps the staircase going.

Counting breaks without it

the arrangement formula forces 0! = 1
80 words, about 36 s

1[HOOK] Zero factorial has to equal one, or counting arrangements breaks.

2[MASCOT: Zero means zero!] Isn't anything with zero in it zero?

3 Factorial counts orders: three books line up in three times two times one, six ways.

4[EQUATION: n! / (n − r)!] Lining up r of n things uses n factorial over n minus r factorial.

5 Line up all n, and the bottom becomes zero factorial.

6 The answer must be n factorial itself, so zero factorial has to be one.

7[TAKEAWAY] Zero factorial is one so the counting formulas stay honest.
