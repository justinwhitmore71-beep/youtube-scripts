[HOOK] Those metal teeth you drive over on bridges are there because the bridge grows. {c4}
[MASCOT: Bridges grow?] Bridges grow?
Warm a solid and it grows slightly; that's called thermal expansion. {c1}
[EQUATION: ΔL = α L ΔT] The stretch is alpha, the material's growth per degree, times the length, times the warming. {c2}
Steel grows twelve millionths of its length per degree. {c2}
Over a span of twelve seventy-five meters and fifty-five degrees, that's eighty-four centimeters. {c3}
[TAKEAWAY] Expansion joints spread that growth into small gaps, so the bridge doesn't buckle. {c4}
