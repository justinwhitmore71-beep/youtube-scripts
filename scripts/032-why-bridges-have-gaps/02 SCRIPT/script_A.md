[MASCOT: Solid steel doesn't stretch!] Wait, solid steel changes size?

[EQUATION: ΔL = α L ΔT] Change in length is alpha, times length, times temperature change. {c2}
Steel's alpha is just twelve millionths per degree, but the bridge is huge. {c2} {c3}

[TAKEAWAY] That's why bridges have expansion joints: gaps that let them grow without buckling. {c4}
