## Description
On ice almost nothing twists a spinning skater, so their amount of spin, L = I × ω, stays fixed. I depends on how far the mass is from the middle, squared, so pulling the arms in shrinks it a lot, and the turning speed ω has to rise to keep L the same. The extra energy comes from the work the arms do pulling in.
