[HOOK] A skater spins much faster the moment their arms come in, with no push. {c3}
[MASCOT: Where does the extra energy come from?] Then where's the extra energy from?
On ice almost nothing twists them, so their amount of spin stays fixed. {c1}
[EQUATION: L = I × ω] That amount is I, how spread out the mass is, times omega, turning speed. {c1} {c2}
Arms in, I shrinks, so omega must climb to compensate. {c3}
Pulling the arms in takes work, and that becomes the extra spin energy. {c4}
[TAKEAWAY] Pull mass inward and the spin speeds up; your muscles pay the energy. {c3} {c4}
