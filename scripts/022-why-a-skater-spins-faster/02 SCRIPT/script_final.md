[HOOK] A spinning skater speeds up just by pulling their arms in. {c3}
[MASCOT: They must push off harder!] Surely they're pushing off the ice harder?
Smooth ice barely twists them, so their amount of spin, L, stays fixed. {c1}
[EQUATION: L = I × ω] Spin is I, how spread out the mass is, times omega, the turning speed. {c1} {c2}
Mass counts by distance squared, so arms pulled halfway in count a quarter. {c2}
I shrinks and L can't change, so omega has to grow. {c3}
[TAKEAWAY] Pull mass inward and the spin must speed up, no push required. {c3}
