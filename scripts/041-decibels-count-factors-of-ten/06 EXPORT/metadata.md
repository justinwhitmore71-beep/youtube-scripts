## Description
The decibel level is β = 10 log₁₀(I/I₀), so it counts factors of ten in intensity (the sound's power per square meter). Every extra 10 dB is ten times the intensity: going from 60 dB to 90 dB is 10 × 10 × 10 = 1000 times as intense. Doubling the intensity adds only about 3 dB.

## Pinned comment (suggested)
'A thousand times the intensity' means a thousand times the sound power reaching each square meter of your ears. How loud it SEEMS to you is a separate question about hearing, which this Short doesn't try to answer.
