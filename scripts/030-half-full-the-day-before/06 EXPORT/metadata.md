## Description
Algae that doubles every day covers a pond on day 30. Run time backwards and it halves each day: half on day 29, a quarter on day 28, only about 3% on day 25. Each doubling adds as much as was already there, so most of the growth happens at the very end. The pond is an invented example of steady doubling.

## Pinned comment (suggested)
Real things rarely double forever: algae runs out of pond, populations run out of food. The point is the shape of steady doubling, where the last few steps do most of the work, which is exactly why it's easy to underestimate.
