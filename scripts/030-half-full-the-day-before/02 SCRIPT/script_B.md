[HOOK] On the day before a doubling pond is full, it's still only half covered. {c3}
[MASCOT: That seems way too late!] Only half, one day before the end?
Steady growth adds the same amount each day; doubling multiplies by the same amount. {c1}
[EQUATION: day 30: 1, day 29: ½, day 28: ¼, day 25: 1/32] So the last day doubles half a pond into a whole one. {c2} {c3}
Five days before the end, it covers just one thirty-second, about three percent. {c3}
Each doubling adds as much as the pond already had. {c4}
[TAKEAWAY] That's why doubling feels sudden: most growth happens at the very end. {c3} {c4}
