Script B

Adding vs doubling

steady growth and doubling side by side
83 words, about 37 s

1[HOOK] On the day before a doubling pond is full, it's still only half covered.

2[MASCOT: That seems way too late!] Only half, one day before the end?

3 Steady growth adds the same amount each day; doubling multiplies by the same amount.

4[EQUATION: day 30: 1, day 29: ½, day 28: ¼, day 25: 1/32] So the last day doubles half a pond into a whole one.

5 Five days before the end, it covers just one thirty-second, about three percent.

6 Each doubling adds as much as the pond already had.

7[TAKEAWAY] That's why doubling feels sudden: most growth happens at the very end.
