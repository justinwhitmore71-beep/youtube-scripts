# Script B: Not a coincidence
Series: Simple Proofs

[HOOK] A triangle, a pentagon, a hexagon: their outside angles all total three sixty. {c3}
[MASCOT: Coincidence?] Is that just a coincidence?
[EQUATION: 3 × 120° = 5 × 72° = 6 × 60° = 360°] Three turns of one twenty, five of seventy-two, six of sixty. {c3}
Each outside angle is how much you turn at a corner as you walk the edge. {c2}
Walk all the way round, and you face exactly where you started. {c2}
That's one full turn, three hundred sixty degrees, for any dent-free shape. {c1} {c2}
[TAKEAWAY] More corners just means smaller turns; one lap is always one full turn. {c2}
