# Script A: One lap, one turn

[HOOK] Walk around any dent-free shape with straight sides, and your turns add up to three hundred sixty degrees. {c2}
[MASCOT: Even a lopsided blob?] Even a lopsided seven-sided blob?
At each corner you turn by some angle, called the exterior angle. {c2}
Back at the start, you face the way you began. {c2}
[EQUATION: sum of exterior angles = 360°] So all your turns together made one full turn: three hundred sixty degrees. {c1} {c2}
Triangle or hexagon, big turns or small, the total never changes. {c2} {c3}
[TAKEAWAY] The corners can change, but one lap is always one full turn. {c2}
