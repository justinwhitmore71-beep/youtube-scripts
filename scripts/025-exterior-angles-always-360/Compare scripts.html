Script A

One lap, one turn

the wrong guess is 'more corners, more angle'
83 words, about 37 s

1[HOOK] Walk around any dent-free shape with straight sides, and your turns add up to three hundred sixty degrees.

2[MASCOT: Even a lopsided blob?] Even a lopsided seven-sided blob?

3 At each corner you turn by some angle, called the exterior angle.

4 Back at the start, you face the way you began.

5[EQUATION: sum of exterior angles = 360°] So all your turns together made one full turn: three hundred sixty degrees.

6 Triangle or hexagon, big turns or small, the total never changes.

7[TAKEAWAY] The corners can change, but one lap is always one full turn.

Script B

Not a coincidence

three regular shapes, then the walk that explains them
84 words, about 37 s

1[HOOK] A triangle, a pentagon, a hexagon: their outside angles all total three sixty.

2[MASCOT: Coincidence?] Is that just a coincidence?

3[EQUATION: 3 × 120° = 5 × 72° = 6 × 60° = 360°] Three turns of one twenty, five of seventy-two, six of sixty.

4 Each outside angle is how much you turn at a corner as you walk the edge.

5 Walk all the way round, and you face exactly where you started.

6 That's one full turn, three hundred sixty degrees, for any dent-free shape.

7[TAKEAWAY] More corners just means smaller turns; one lap is always one full turn.
