5. Triangle, pentagon, hexagon: all 360°

## Description
Walk around a straight-sided shape with no dents and turn at each corner: those turns are the exterior angles. When you're back at the start you face the way you began, so all the turns together made exactly one full turn, 360°. A triangle has big turns and a hexagon small ones, but the total is always 360°.

## Pinned comment (suggested)
For shapes with dents, it still works if you count a turn the other way as negative: you still finish facing the way you started, after one net full turn. The video sticks to dent-free shapes to keep it simple.
