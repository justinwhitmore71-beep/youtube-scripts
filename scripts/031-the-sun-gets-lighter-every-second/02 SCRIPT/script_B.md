[MASCOT: Light weighs something?] Light can carry mass away?
[EQUATION: E = m c²] Einstein's E equals m c squared says energy and mass come as a package. {c1}
One kilogram of mass matches nine times ten to the sixteen joules. {c5}
So pouring out four times ten to the twenty-six joules a second costs four billion kilograms. {c2} {c3}
That sounds huge, but the Sun can keep it up for billions of years. {c4}
[TAKEAWAY] Sunlight is the Sun slowly turning its own mass into light. {c1} {c3}
