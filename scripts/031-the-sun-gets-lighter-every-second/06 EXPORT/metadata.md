## Description
Einstein's E = mc² says energy and mass are linked. The Sun shines out about 4 × 10²⁶ joules every second; divide by c², the speed of light squared, and that's about 4 billion kilograms, roughly 4 million tons, of mass leaving the Sun each second. Because c² is so huge, the Sun has enough mass to keep this up for billions of years.

## Pinned comment (suggested)
The mass isn't 'burned' like fuel in a fire: the Sun's energy comes from converting mass into energy as it fuses hydrogen, and that energy leaves as light. 4 million tons a second sounds huge, but the Sun is so massive it can keep this up for billions of years.
