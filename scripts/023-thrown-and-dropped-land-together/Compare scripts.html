Script A

Sideways buys no airtime

the wrong guess is 'the thrown one stays up longer'
82 words, about 36 s

1[HOOK] Drop one ball and throw another sideways at the same moment: they land together.

2[MASCOT: Surely the fast one flies longer?] Doesn't the thrown ball stay up longer?

3 Ignoring air, gravity only pulls down, and it pulls both the same.

4 Sideways speed carries the thrown ball farther, but adds nothing to its fall.

5[EQUATION: y = ½ g t²] Both fall one half g t squared in the same time.

6 So the thrown ball just lands farther away, at the very same instant.

7[TAKEAWAY] Sideways and downward motion are separate, so sideways speed never buys airtime.

Script B

Same height, every frame

the strobe photo is the proof
81 words, about 36 s

1[HOOK] A ball shot sideways and a ball simply dropped hit the floor at the same instant.

2[MASCOT: That can't be right!] But the thrown one is going so much faster?

3 Take flash photos as they fall, and both are at the same height in every frame.

4[EQUATION: y = ½ g t²] Their drop depends only on time falling: one half g t squared.

5 The sideways speed changes where the ball lands, not when.

6 That's because, without air, gravity pulls only straight down.

7[TAKEAWAY] Speed sideways never buys extra time in the air.
