Script A

Fifty down, fifty up

the wrong guess is 'they cancel'
85 words, about 38 s

1[HOOK] Lose half your money, gain fifty percent back, and you're still down a quarter.

2[MASCOT: Isn't fifty minus fifty zero?] Down fifty, up fifty: shouldn't that cancel?

3 A percent is always a percent of the amount you're starting from.

4 Start with a hundred dollars: losing half leaves fifty.

5[EQUATION: 100 × 0.5 × 1.5 = 75] Fifty percent of fifty is only twenty-five, so you climb back to seventy-five.

6 The gain came from a smaller amount, so it can't fill the hole.

7[TAKEAWAY] Equal percent moves don't cancel: after a big loss, you need an even bigger gain.

Script B

Up, then down, then cheaper

the same trap in the other order
85 words, about 38 s

1[HOOK] Raise a price fifty percent, then cut it fifty percent, and it ends up cheaper.

2[MASCOT: Up and down the same?] Wait, same percent up and down?

3 Each percent is taken of the current price.

4[EQUATION: 100 × 1.5 × 0.5 = 75] A hundred dollars rises to one fifty; half of that is seventy-five.

5 That cut was half a bigger number, so it removed more than the raise added.

6 Either order ends at seventy-five: one point five times a half is three quarters.

7[TAKEAWAY] Percent changes multiply rather than add, so equal ups and downs never cancel.
