5. Percentages multiply, they don't add

## Description
A percent change is always a percent of the amount at that moment. Halve $100 and you have $50; a 50% gain on $50 is only $25, so you end at $75. It works the same in the other order: $100 up 50% is $150, and half of that is $75. Percent changes multiply (0.5 × 1.5 = 0.75), so equal ups and downs don't cancel.

## Pinned comment (suggested)
This isn't a trick of wording: any time a percent is taken of the current amount (sale prices, savings, investments), equal ups and downs leave you below where you started. To undo a 50% drop you need a 100% rise.
