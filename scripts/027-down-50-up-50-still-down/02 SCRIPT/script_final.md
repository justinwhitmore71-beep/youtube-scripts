# Script A: Fifty down, fifty up

[HOOK] Lose half your money, gain fifty percent back, and you're still down a quarter. {c3}
[MASCOT: Isn't fifty minus fifty zero?] Down fifty, up fifty: shouldn't that cancel?
A percent is always a percent of the amount you're starting from. {c1} {c2}
Start with a hundred dollars: losing half leaves fifty. {c3}
[EQUATION: 100 × 0.5 × 1.5 = 75] Fifty percent of fifty is only twenty-five, so you climb back to seventy-five. {c3}
The gain came from a smaller amount, so it can't fill the hole. {c3}
[TAKEAWAY] Equal percent moves don't cancel: after a big loss, you need an even bigger gain. {c3} {c4}
