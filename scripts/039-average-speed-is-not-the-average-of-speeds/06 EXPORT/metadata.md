## Description
Drive 60 km at 30 km/h and come back at 90 km/h. The way there takes 2 hours and the way back 40 minutes, so you cover 120 km in 2 hours 40 minutes: an average of 45 km/h, not 60. Average speed is total distance over total time, and you spend three times as long at the slow speed. The trip is an invented example.

## Pinned comment (suggested)
The 60 km doesn't matter: pick any distance and the average still comes out at 45 km/h. And here's the twist: if you went there at 30, no return speed, however fast, can bring your average up to 60.
