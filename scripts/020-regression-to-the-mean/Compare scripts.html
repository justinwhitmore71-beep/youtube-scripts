The pep talk that did nothing

the wrong guess is 'my fix worked'

1[HOOK] Pick the worst scorers, do nothing at all, and on average they improve next time.

2[MASCOT: So my pep talk worked?] Wait, didn't my pep talk fix them?

3[EQUATION: score = skill + luck] Every score is part skill and part luck on the day.

4 The worst scores usually include a big helping of bad luck.

5 Luck doesn't repeat, so their next scores drift back toward the middle.

6 That's regression to the mean, and it happens even when nothing changed.

7[TAKEAWAY] Extremes drift back on their own, so don't credit a fix for what luck undid.

3[EQUATION: score = skill + luck] Every score mixes real skill with some luck on the day.

4 The very top scores were boosted by unusually good luck.

5 Next week the skill stays, but the luck is fresh, so scores slide toward average.

6 The same pull lifts the bottom scorers, and it's called regression to the mean.
