## Description
Pick the people with the worst scores, do nothing, and on average they score better next time. Every score is partly skill and partly luck on the day; the worst scores usually had bad luck, and luck doesn't carry over, so the next scores drift back toward average. This is regression to the mean, and it can make useless fixes look like they worked.

## Pinned comment (suggested)
This is 'on average', not for every person: some low scorers will do even worse. And it doesn't mean everyone ends up average; the skill part stays. It only pulls back the part of an extreme score that was chance.
