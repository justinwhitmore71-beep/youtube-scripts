## Description
Ten is 9 + 1, a hundred is 99 + 1, a thousand is 999 + 1. So any number splits into a pile of nines plus the sum of its digits: 738 = 7×99 + 3×9 + (7 + 3 + 8). The pile always divides by 9, so the digit sum alone decides whether the whole number does.

## Pinned comment (suggested)
The same reason gives the rule for 3, because 9 is a multiple of 3. It does NOT work for 7: 16 has digit sum 7, but 16 isn't divisible by 7. Ten is one more than nine, not one more than seven, and that's what makes nine special.
