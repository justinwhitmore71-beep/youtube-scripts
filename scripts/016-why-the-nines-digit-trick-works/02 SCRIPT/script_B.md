[HOOK] You can tell if a huge number divides by nine by adding its digits. {c1}
[MASCOT: That sounds like a magic trick.] Is that a trick, or real maths?
Ten is nine plus one, and a hundred is ninety-nine plus one. {c2}
So each digit carries a pile of nines, plus itself. {c2}
[EQUATION: 4572 = (multiple of 9) + 4 + 5 + 7 + 2] So four thousand five hundred seventy-two is a pile of nines, plus four, five, seven, two. {c2}
Those make eighteen, a multiple of nine, so the number is too. {c1} {c2}
[TAKEAWAY] The digit trick isn't magic; it's ten being one more than nine. {c2}
