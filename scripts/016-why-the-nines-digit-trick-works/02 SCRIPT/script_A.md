# Script A: Ten is nine plus one
Series: Simple Proofs
Angle: one number split into nines and ones
Suggested [MINE] line: line 6 (rewrite it in your own words, or keep it, and put [MINE] at its start; keep its beat words so the animation still lands)

[HOOK] Add up a number's digits, and you instantly know if nine divides it. {c1}
[MASCOT: Why would digits know that?] Why would the digits know anything about nine?
Take seven hundred thirty-eight: seven hundreds, three tens, and eight ones. {c2}
[EQUATION: 738 = 7×99 + 3×9 + (7 + 3 + 8)] Every hundred is ninety-nine plus one, and every ten is nine plus one. {c2}
So it's a pile of nines plus seven, plus three, plus eight. {c2}
The pile always splits into nines, so only the digit sum, eighteen, decides. {c2}
[TAKEAWAY] Nine works because ten is just one more than nine. {c2}
