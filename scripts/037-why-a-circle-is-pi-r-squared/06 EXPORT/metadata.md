## Description
A pizza twice as wide has four times the area. Cut a circle into thin slices and line them up, tips alternating: the shape gets closer and closer to a rectangle whose height is the radius r and whose width is half the circumference, πr. So the area is r × πr = πr². Double the radius and both sides double, so the area goes up four times.

## Pinned comment (suggested)
With any real number of slices the edges stay a little wavy; the rectangle is exact only in the limit of infinitely thin slices. That 'take thinner and thinner pieces' idea is exactly what calculus makes precise.
