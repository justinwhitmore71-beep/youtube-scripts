[HOOK] A pizza twice as wide has four times as much pizza. {c1} {c6}
[MASCOT: Twice as wide, twice as much?] Shouldn't twice as wide mean twice as much?
Slice it thin and line the slices up, tips alternating. {c3}
Thinner slices get closer to a rectangle. {c3}
Its height is the radius; its width, half the crust, is pi times the radius. {c2} {c3}
[EQUATION: A = πr × r = πr²] So the area is radius times pi times radius: pi r squared. {c1} {c3}
Double the radius and both sides double, so the area quadruples. {c6}
[TAKEAWAY] That's the squared in pi r squared: size counts twice. {c6}
