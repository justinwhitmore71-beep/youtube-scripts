[HOOK] Double a circle's radius, and its area doesn't double; it quadruples. {c6}
[MASCOT: Why four times?] Why four times, not two?
Peel the circle into thin rings and straighten each one. {c5}
They stack into a triangle: base two pi r, the outer crust, and height r. {c2} {c5}
[EQUATION: A = ½ × 2πr × r = πr²] Half base times height: half of two pi r, times r, pi r squared. {c4} {c5}
Double r, and the triangle doubles in both base and height. {c6}
[TAKEAWAY] Two doublings make four times the area: that's what the squared means. {c6}
