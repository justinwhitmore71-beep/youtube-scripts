## Description
Nine people earn $40,000 and one earns $10 million. The mean (the usual average) adds everything and divides by ten: $1,036,000, more than anyone but one person earns. The median lines everyone up and takes the middle: $40,000. Extreme values drag the mean but barely move the median. The numbers are an invented example.

## Pinned comment (suggested)
Neither number is 'wrong': the mean tells you the total shared out equally, the median tells you about the person in the middle. They answer different questions, and they only disagree this much when a few values are extreme.
