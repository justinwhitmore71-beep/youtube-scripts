Adding forever, getting one

1[HOOK] Add a half, a quarter, an eighth, forever, and you get exactly one.

2[MASCOT: Adding forever stays finite?] How can adding forever not give infinity?

3 Walk to a door: half the way, then half what's left, and so on.

4[EQUATION: 1/2 + 1/4 + 1/8 + ... = 1] Each step halves the gap: a half left, a quarter, an eighth.

5 The gap shrinks toward zero, so the total of the steps closes in on exactly one.

6 That's Zeno's ancient puzzle, and why you really reach the door.

7[TAKEAWAY] Infinitely many pieces can still add up to something finite.
