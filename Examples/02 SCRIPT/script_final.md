# Script A: Exactly six

Series: Simple Proofs
Angle: the claim, then the triangles

\[HOOK] Put coins around a coin of the same size, and exactly six fit, with no gaps and no wiggle room. {c1}
\[MASCOT: Why not seven?] Why exactly six and not seven?
Join the centers of the middle coin and two neighbors. {c1}
Each side is two radii long, so the triangle has three equal sides. {c1}
\[MINE] Equal sides mean equal corners: sixty degrees each. {c2}
Six of those sixty-degree slices make a full circle around the middle. {c1}
\[TAKEAWAY] Six coins, because six times sixty is three sixty.
