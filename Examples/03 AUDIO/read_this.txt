Put coins around a coin of the same size, and exactly six fit, with no gaps and no wiggle room.

Why exactly six and not seven?

Join the centers of the middle coin and two neighbors.

Each side is two radii long, so the triangle has three equal sides.

Equal sides mean equal corners: sixty degrees each.

Six of those sixty-degree slices make a full circle around the middle.

Six coins, because six times sixty is three sixty.
