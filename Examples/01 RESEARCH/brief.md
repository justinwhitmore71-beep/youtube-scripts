# Brief: why six coins fit around one

Series: Simple Proofs
Idea: Equal coins touching a middle coin have centres two radii apart, so any two neighbours and the middle make an equilateral triangle with 60-degree corners; 360 / 60 = 6 coins exactly.
Misconception: The number that fits around a coin is just whatever happens; maybe it depends on the coin.
Hook angle: Exactly six equal coins fit around one, with no gap.
Narrowed to: Equal circles in a flat plane only.
Key condition to say aloud: all coins the same size, lying flat.
Candidate sources: Proven on screen (pure math)
Originality: The six triangles fan out from the middle coin and close the full turn.
Not used: Kissing numbers in 3D (12); hexagonal packing.
