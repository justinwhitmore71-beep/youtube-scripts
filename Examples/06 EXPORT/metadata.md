## Description
Put coins around a coin of the same size and exactly six fit, touching with no gaps. Join the centres of the middle coin and two neighbours: each side is two radii long, so the triangle has three equal sides and three 60-degree corners. Six of those 60-degree slices make a full circle: 6 x 60 = 360.
